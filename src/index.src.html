A coin flip, every 15 minutes.

BTC 15 follows a real yes-or-no market: will Bitcoin close above the target price in 15 minutes? It keeps score around the clock.

Just for fun. No bets, no money, no advice.

The game

  1. A round opensEvery 15 minutes. The target is the price the last round closed at.
  2. Call itYES to close above the target. NO to close below.
  3. The market settles itThe result comes from the exchange. The app only keeps score.

The market is Kalshi's 15-minute Bitcoin contract, the one behind Robinhood's "BTC 15 min". The app reads its public feed. No account, no key.

Three callers, every round

  • YouTap YES or NO before the round locks.
  • ALGOBacks the market's favourite. It calls with 12, 10, 5 and about 3 minutes left.
  • History CallLooks up what came after the last three results.

On around the clock

The marketKalshi's public feed. Target, odds and result.
One Docker containerOne Python file and one SQLite database.
Any browserThree pages. Close them all and it keeps recording.
  • No browser needed. The server settles each round and logs every call itself, day and night.
  • Nothing to install. Plain Python, no extra packages.
  • History survives. The database sits on a Docker volume, so restarts and updates keep it.
  • Gaps fill themselves. After a restart it pulls any missed rounds from the market's public history.
